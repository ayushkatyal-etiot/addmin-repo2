Office Home

Current obligations, pending actions, overdue items, upcoming renewals, and setup completion for an office — in one view, on live data.

My Actions queue

Every pending item across Utility, Lease, Maintenance, Asset, Vendor, and Compliance modules, aggregated into a single personal queue per user.

Executive Dashboard with drill-down

Always live, never seeded

No dummy or hard-coded KPI values appear in any environment a real user sees. A newly activated office with no history shows a getting-started state, not a misleading zero chart.

The view that gets renewal sponsored

Admin teams need a queue they can act from without hopping between module menus. Executives need a single cross-office picture of spend, overdue items, and compliance risk without asking Admin for a manual report every month. AddMin gives both, from the same live data.

For the Office Admin

  • Land on Office Home and see what’s due, overdue, and expiring for your office, plus setup completion if it’s still onboarding.
  • Open My Actions and act directly on any pending item — no navigating through module menus first.

For the Office Head / CXO

  • Switch to the Executive Dashboard, filter by office, module, or period — filters persist in the URL so views are shareable.
  • Click any KPI to drill into the source records behind it.
  • Export any filtered view to PDF or Excel.
