## Introduction

AddMin ("we", "us") provides an office administration platform for organizations managing utilities, leases, compliance, vendors, and assets across offices. This policy describes what data we collect and how it's used.

## Information We Collect

### Account & Organization Data

* Organization name, GSTIN, and office addresses
* User name, email address, and role/office assignments
* Authentication data, including MFA enrollment status

### Operational Data

* Utility bills, lease and landlord records, vendor and AMC contracts
* Compliance documents and certificate metadata
* Payment records (date, amount, mode, reference) — AddMin does not process payments directly

## How We Use Data

Operational data is used exclusively to power obligation tracking, approval workflows, and dashboards for the organization that owns it. Data is never shared across organizations.

## Data Retention & Deletion

Audit trail records (approvals, payments, access logs) are retained for compliance purposes even after a record is closed or archived. Contact us to request account data deletion, subject to statutory retention requirements.
